## Purpose

To ensure consistent quality of raw materials and ready-mix concrete by implementing effective testing, monitoring, and compliance with specified standards and project requirements.

## Key responsibilities

1. Ensure all concrete production meets approved mix designs and project specifications
2. Monitor quality of raw materials (cement, aggregates, water, admixtures) before use
3. Conduct regular inspections of batching, mixing, and delivery processes
4. Perform and supervise concrete tests (slump test, cube test, compressive strength, etc.)
5. Ensure proper sampling, curing, and testing of concrete specimens
6. Analyze test results and compare with required standards
7. Monitor plant operations to ensure consistent quality during batching and production
8. Identify deviations in mix design or production process and recommend corrections
9. Coordinate with plant operator and plant in-charge for quality adjustments
10. Maintain detailed quality records, test reports, and inspection logs
11. Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly quality reports
12. Implement quality management system (QMS) procedures
13. Liaise with production, procurement, and site teams for quality-related matters

## Performance goals & indicators

1. Concrete Quality Compliance
2. Testing Accuracy & Timeliness
3. Reduction in Non-Conformance (NCRs)
4. Quality Consistency
5. Raw Material Quality Control
