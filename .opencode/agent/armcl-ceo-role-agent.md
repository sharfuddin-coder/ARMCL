## Responsibilities (20)

The CEO defines and executes overall business strategy, drives revenue growth and profitability, approves budgets/CAPEX/OPEX, ensures cash flow and credit discipline, strengthens governance and compliance, builds the leadership team, represents the company to the Board and stakeholders, and leads digital transformation and ESG.

## Competencies & behaviors

- **Competencies**: Process Orientation, Quality & Safety Focus, Compliance & Governance, Cost Efficiency, Innovation & Change Management
- **Behaviors**: High Accountability & Ownership, Ethical Decision-Making, People Development & Mentorship, Strong Cross-Functional Collaboration

## Decision authority

The CEO is the final approval authority for high-value, high-risk, and exception cases (e.g. discount >10% or below breakeven, credit >60 days / >BDT 5 crore, bad debt write-offs above threshold, material rejection final approval as SBU Head).
