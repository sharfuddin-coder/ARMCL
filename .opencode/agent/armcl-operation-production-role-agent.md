## Production roles

### 1. Factory Incharge (Plant In-Charge)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Department / Section | Operations / Operations |
| Location | Dhour, Rupganj (Narayanganj), Mawna (Gazipur), Kalurghat (Chattogram) |
| Immediate supervisor | CEO - ABSL |
| Next level supervisor | Deputy COO |
| Direct reports | 3 — Production Head, Quality Head, Maintenance Head |
| Education / Experience | Diploma or BSc in Civil Engineering · 5-7 years |

**Purpose**: Ensure the safe, efficient, and cost-effective operation of the ready-mix concrete plant while delivering high-quality products on time and meeting business and customer requirements.

**Key responsibilities** (15 total, incl.):
1. Coordinate daily plant operations for uninterrupted production
2. Plan and schedule batching per project demands and delivery timelines
3. Ensure optimal utilization of plant capacity, machinery, and workforce
4. Ensure all products meet required standards (strength, durability, workability)
5. Coordinate preventive and corrective maintenance of plant, mixers, transit mixers
6. Liaise with clients, contractors, and site engineers
7. Monitor production costs (materials, fuel, labor, maintenance)
8. Control wastage and improve operational efficiency

**KPIs**: Production Efficiency · Product Quality Compliance · On-Time Delivery Performance · Plant Downtime Reduction · Cost Control & Waste Reduction

### 3. Batching Plant Operator

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Department / Section | Operations / Production |
| Immediate supervisor | Production Head |
| Next level supervisor | Factory Incharge |
| Direct reports | 1 — Production Helper |
| Education / Experience | Diploma in Civil Engineering · 3-5 years |

**Purpose**: Operate the batching plant efficiently and safely to produce accurate, high-quality ready-mix concrete in line with approved mix designs and production schedules.

**Key responsibilities** (18 total, incl.):
1. Operate the batching plant to produce concrete per approved mix designs
2. Control and monitor the batching process (cement, aggregates, water, admixtures)
3. Ensure accurate weighing and proportioning of materials
4. Receive and execute production orders from plant in-charge / Production Head
5. Adjust production based on site requirements and delivery schedules
6. Ensure consistency of concrete mixes during batching
7. Monitor slump, workability, and report deviations
8. Coordinate with lab/quality team for testing and corrections

**KPIs**: Batching Accuracy · Production Target Achievement · On-Time Delivery Performance · Quality Consistency · Plant Downtime Control

### 4. Wheel Loader Operator

(from production policy responsibilities)
- Loads raw materials, maintains stockyard, performs safety checks, follows protocols, supports plant operations, and reports equipment issues promptly

## Rules (apply to all production roles)

- Only authorized, trained personnel may operate or adjust the batching system
- Operations stop immediately on unsafe conditions
- All operational, quality, and maintenance records archived for audits
