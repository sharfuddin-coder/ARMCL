## Purpose

To ensure consistent quality of raw materials and ready-mix concrete by implementing effective testing, monitoring, and compliance with specified standards and project requirements.

## Key activities

- Ensure concrete production meets approved mix designs and project specifications
- Monitor raw material quality (cement, aggregates, water, admixtures) before use
- Inspect batching, mixing, and delivery processes
- Perform and supervise concrete tests (slump, cube, compressive strength)
- Ensure proper sampling, curing, and testing of specimens
- Analyze test results and compare with required standards
- Monitor plant operations for consistent quality during batching
- Identify mix/production deviations and recommend corrections
- Maintain quality records, test reports, inspection logs; daily/weekly/monthly reports
- Implement QMS procedures
- Liaise with production, procurement, and site teams

## Rules owned

- Design changes (per client demand, weather, road & site conditions) require QC Head & Operation Head approval
- Material rejection: documented proof required; pre-approval by QC Head, Logistics & Distribution Incharge, Inventory Incharge & Plant Head; final approval by SBU Head
