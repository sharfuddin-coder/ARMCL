## Purpose of the Position

Provide overall strategic, operational, and financial leadership for Akij Readymix Concrete Ltd. Ensure sustainable growth, profitability, and market leadership in the Bangladesh ready-mix concrete / building materials industry. Translate Board directives into executable business strategies while ensuring governance, compliance, and organizational excellence.

---

## Key Responsibilities and Accountabilities

1. Define and execute the overall business strategy, annual operating plan, and long-term growth roadmap.
2. Lead revenue growth, market share expansion, and profitability improvement across all regions and channels.
3. Ensure optimal utilization of manufacturing capacity, supply chain efficiency, and cost leadership.
4. Oversee sales, marketing, operations, finance, HR, SCM, IT, and support functions to ensure alignment with corporate goals.
5. Drive net profit improvement through pricing discipline, cost optimization, working capital control, and portfolio mix.
6. Approve annual budgets, CAPEX, OPEX, and major investment proposals.
7. Ensure strong cash flow management, credit discipline, BG compliance, and risk mitigation.
8. Monitor production planning, clinker strategy, energy optimization (AFR), and logistics efficiency.
9. Strengthen governance, internal controls, audit compliance, and regulatory adherence.
10. Build and lead a high-performance leadership team with clear KPIs, accountability, and succession planning.
11. Represent the company before the Board, regulators, banks, strategic partners, and key stakeholders.
12. Ensure brand reputation, ethical conduct, and compliance with Akij Group values.
13. Lead digital transformation, MIS governance, and data-driven decision-making culture.
14. Review market intelligence, competitor strategy, and industry trends to ensure proactive responses.
15. Oversee customer satisfaction, dealer/partner relationships, and key project accounts.
16. Manage crisis situations related to supply disruption, market volatility, regulatory changes, or reputational risk.
17. Approve and monitor major trade programs, pricing corridors, and incentive structures.
18. Ensure organizational capability development through training, leadership development, and talent retention.
19. Coordinate with Cluster CEO and other Akij concerns to drive synergy and shared value creation.
20. Uphold long-term sustainability, ESG principles, and responsible manufacturing practices.
